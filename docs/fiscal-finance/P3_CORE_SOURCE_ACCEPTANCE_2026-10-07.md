## Decision and remaining Gate 2 evidence

For a compact central-source pilot shortlist, the Treasury debt-statistics feed has the strongest combination of topical fit, 10/10 observed body extraction, two-round list idempotency, and a separately observed older page. The policy feed is a reasonable second source with the same 10-row / two-round / 10-body sample, but its broad lottery content and older last-observed homepage need explicit editorial review. OMO provides useful evidence of daily-window movement and table preservation, but its article/body sample is only two notices and it has no history-page evidence.

The existing batches share an important limitation: their request-level hard hop counters failed to observe the original collector fetches. The reports preserve this as unknown; later successful budget-tool work does not retroactively prove those requests stayed under their intended hop limit. Same-day duplicate collection, immediate repeat idempotency, or one next-day difference do not establish outage recovery, persistent daily polling, complete rolling-window coverage, or source admission.

All three industry sources remain `enabled=false` and both full-text flags remain false. Their bounded evidence closes several identity, two-round duplicate, and sample-body checks already recorded in P3; it does not close G2-A2/A3/A5/A6 or the project-wide 35-local-bureau requirement. Gate 2 remains `NOT_PASSED`, source admission remains `NOT_ADMITTED`, and coverage remains unproven.
